The Hall · Records

Round records

Every round of Cup & Claim and Sealed Hand has a public number: the game, the table, the round. The table shows it as “Table 3 · Round 41”. At Cup & Claim, a fingerprint of the dice is posted here before they're seen, and the secrets are opened when the round ends, so anyone can recheck the dice. At Sealed Hand, a seal over the whole shuffled deck is posted before any card is dealt, and the seed and the deck open when the round ends, so anyone can rebuild every hand and recheck every claim's verdict.
