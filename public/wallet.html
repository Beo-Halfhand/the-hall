Standing is a request, and it ends your permit. The next settle frees your seat, or the table goes quiet and frees it for you.

The judge can’t seat or move you after this, until you grant it again. A seat you already hold stays until it’s settled, released or stood from. Nothing the judge does can stop a revoke.

Your player key

Your key is your seat in the cage. Anyone who has it can sit you at a table and put your balance at risk. They can’t send it anywhere but your own address. Keep it private. Never shown in addresses or logs.

Lost your key?

Your wallet can’t rebuild it, but your deposit isn’t lost. The cage remembers your address, and anyone can send a balance home once its seat is free.
